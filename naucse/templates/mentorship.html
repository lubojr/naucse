Mentorship programme

About the programme

This programme is aiming on connecting mentees with mentors based on possible shared professional interests, desired skills development, and personality compatibility.

With finishing a Beginners Python course your coding journey does not stop! Now you are familiar with building blocks and it is on you to decide in which directions to go. And mentors and our mentorship programme can help you. It would be great if you are able to define what you are looking for, but if you just want a learning buddy it is also fine.

For whom is the mentoring programme?

  • You are a woman or from underrepresented genders.
  • You are looking for someone with specific knowledge in our tech field who can help you achieve your goals.
  • You want to continue studying and want a buddy who will hold you accountable and help with tips and tricks when needed.
  • You want to land a job in IT and want someone to help you on this journey.
  • You want to jump from junior to medior and build a skills (not only technical) needed.

Important information

  • Programme will start with an offline kickoff event in Vienna during 1st week of June. Activities include introductions, program overview, initial training sessions, and goal-setting exercises. You will have a chance to get to know other participants!
  • Guaranteed at least five sessions over the duration of the program. Sessions can be a mix of face-to-face and online meetings, ensuring flexibility. This is up to the decision of mentorship pairs.
  • A 15-minute pre-program call for mentees, ensuring they understand the program's structure, expectations, and their objectives. We can offer the same for the mentors.
  • The programme will be finished by a final presentation scheduled for the end of September. It will be a hybrid event in Vienna where mentees showcase their achievements and learning outcomes.
  • Program Fee: €250 for mentees, partially covering administrative costs and resources and rewards mentors for their focused time.
  • We can offer scholarship for those who can not afford it. If that is your case, please send us an email describing your situation.

How to join as a mentee and how does it work?

  1. As a mentee, fill out the google form https://forms.gle/2wRLMfEp7YNSN9qx8 with your details, motivation and what you are interested in.
  2. We will reach out before the course start to arrange a 15 minute call to talk about your expectations. We will also send you all necessary information about the programme and payment.

How to join as a mentor?

As a mentor, fill out the google form https://forms.gle/UK12S7t6vxg2p9iq8 with your details and a list of experience you can provide. We will reach out before the course start to let you know about possible assigned mentee(s).
